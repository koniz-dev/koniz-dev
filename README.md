## 👋 About

I enjoy taking a product from a rough idea to something people can use: shaping
the interface, designing the backend, and making the deployment reliable. My
public work is a mix of reusable tools, product experiments, and things I have
learned while working across independent and collaborative codebases.

At the moment, I am going deeper on DevOps and AWS while keeping my
problem-solving skills sharp through data structures and algorithms.
